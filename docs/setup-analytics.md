# Google Analytics 4 Setup Guide

## How to Get GA4 Measurement ID:

1. **Create Google Analytics 4 Property:**
   - Go to: https://analytics.google.com
   - Sign in with your Google account
   - Click "Admin" (gear icon) → "Create Account"

2. **Set Up Account:**
   - Account Name: "MNS Bank Bhopal"
   - Property Name: "MNS Bank Website"
   - Reporting Time Zone: "India"
   - Currency: "Indian Rupee (₹)"

3. **Create Property:**
   - Select "Web" platform
   - Website URL: "https://mnsbankbhopal.com"
   - Stream Name: "Website Stream"

4. **Get Your Measurement ID:**
   - After creation, you'll see your Measurement ID (G-XXXXXXXXXX)
   - Format: G-XXXXXXXXXX (13 characters starting with G-)

## Example GA4 ID (for testing):
NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID=G-XXXXXXXXXX

⚠️  Replace with your actual GA4 Measurement ID from Google Analytics!
